# Screen Capture Benchmark

No public durability claim is allowed until a real provider is integrated and
measured.

The benchmark must cover native Windows, Android, and iPhone screenshots; PNG,
JPEG, and WebP; partial crops; scaling; brightness and contrast changes;
Messenger, Facebook, Instagram, and text recompression; monitor and phone-screen
photos across angles and lighting; multiple marked cards; unmarked negatives;
altered codes; and wrong-partner attacks.

Report detection rate separately from authoritative-resolution rate. The release
invariant is zero incorrect payable partner assignments across negative and
ambiguity tests.
